## Scope and overlap assessment

The [current cash PP&E primitive](financial-methodology.md) includes evidenced
capitalized operational software in a proven purchase perimeter. It is neither
a tangible-only definition nor automatic approval of all productive assets,
purchased rights, acquired intangibles or issuer extensions.

- **PP&E/software:** all five policies record buildings, equipment and related
  improvements at cost; construction costs including interest and acquisition/
  development of software may be capitalized. Development costs that fail
  capitalization criteria are expensed. This is affirmative recognition evidence
  for eligible assets, not a cash split or an exhaustive definition of the entire
  reported purchase line. MD&A business/interconnected-customer investment
  descriptions establish purpose, not component completeness.
- **Acquisitions:** business-purchase cash is a separate investing line. Selected
  acquisition notes distinguish purchase-price allocations from ordinary cash
  purchases. No acquisition-origin software, PP&E, goodwill or intangible fair
  value is added to or subtracted from Capex. The expressly reported zero business
  cash fact for fiscal 2022 is evidence for that line, not inferred zero for every
  other excluded asset category.
  The HD Supply notes in the fiscal 2021/2022 selected filings discuss the
  December 2020 acquisition, not a mapping of the fiscal 2021 $421 million
  business-purchase cash line; no cross-period acquisition attribution is made.
- **Leases:** each property/lease note separates operating cash, finance-lease
  operating cash, finance-lease financing principal, and noncash ROU additions.
  Finance-leased assets can be in PP&E without being cash investing purchases.
  These disclosures rule out blindly adding asset additions or principal, but do
  not supply an exhaustive purchase-cash mapping.
- **Noncash additions:** the cash-flow supplemental noncash Capex line is tagged
  `CapitalLeaseObligationsIncurred`. It is not the cash candidate and is not the
  same reported amount as new finance-lease ROU assets in the lease note. Different
  concepts/presentations are not duplicate occurrences; no unexplained difference
  is allocated, netted or used as a residual derivation.
- **Proceeds/netting/timing:** other investing activities is aggregated. No
  absence-as-zero PP&E disposal, incentive, unpaid-purchase or standalone
  intangible-payment assumption is permitted. The reviewed cash subtotal is not
  reconstructed from changes in property/software balances or from investing
  totals. Gross/net and complete asset scope require affirmative linkage rather
  than treating an unqualified label as proof.
- **Extensions:** extension facts in the supplement are support only, separately
  gated. No issuer extension, generic fallback or software/acquisition adjustment
  equation is approved.
