## Population contract

| Population | Acceptance and boundary |
| --- | --- |
| Current reported liquidity | Independently displayed standard cash/equivalents plus current marketable securities in the same current-assets panel, or its evidenced reported parent. Both children are required; absent investments are not zero. Broader ShortTermInvestments requires the existing exact reviewed current-liquidity note, with its unchanged digest/excerpt; that review is not extended to other filings. This is not unrestricted/excess cash or certification of every asset's liquidity. |
| Borrowing | Explicit standard candidate with a total-debt/borrowing caption is retained as a partial reported observation. Caption/tag alone cannot establish carrying versus principal basis or complete short-term/lease inclusion. Current/long-term-only captions, fair value and combined capital-lease amounts cannot substitute. No complete borrowing/finance-lease sum is made. |
| Finance and operating leases | Recognized discounted reported liability parent, or complete current/noncurrent partition in one disclosure. The two lease types remain separate; these are not undiscounted maturities, lease cash payments or an economic lease convention. |
| Issuer common shares | Exact nondimensional DEI cover fact independently labelled unclassed common shares, actual observation date/unit/precision. Classed populations or multiple cover populations are retained for review, never summed or replaced with one class. No weighted-average/diluted share estimate. |

Long-term, strategic/equity-method, restricted and combined populations remain in
the attached original component inventory with overlaps and alternatives. An
extension's local name does not grant standard semantics. Displayed populations
with conflicting values/precision, invalid bindings or ambiguous partitions stay
unselected. Complete means the **expressly defined reported population**, not a
complete balance sheet, economic bridge or financially sufficient company.
The old null requirements remain visible; a newly usable narrow population does
not automatically retire broader/perimeter requirements or original blockers.

## Independent CFO check

`displayed-cfo-reconciliation-v1` enumerates the exact current annual display
between a unique starting subtotal and reported CFO. Every displayed numeric row
must bind independently to the retained instance and the primary's own namespace,
CIK, context, unit, precision, lexical sign and scale. Comparative, dimensional,
nil, wrong-currency and unbound rows cannot be used. Confirming occurrences and
competing/invalid populations remain in the proof.

Intermediate income/CFO subtotals and known movement parent/children cannot be
added alongside their children. Consolidated/continuing scopes stay distinct.
Missing rows, ambiguous hierarchy, multiple populations or invalid bindings make
the check PARTIAL, not green. No qualifying independent statement makes it
NOT_ASSESSED. Only a complete, non-overlapping independently enumerated population
is summed. Difference against separately reported CFO is FAILED outside the sum
of each operand/CFO's half-unit rounding interval; INF contributes zero. This is
an absolute filing-precision bound, not an arbitrary percentage.

For a SUPPORTED consolidated statement, a separate reported CFO confirmation and
its displayed rows are delivered. The optional unclassified remainder is CFO
minus exact identified starting/adjustment/movement operands; all other displayed
rows remain observable. It is explicitly partial, not an independent validation,
complete working capital, organic cash flow or an approved FCFF adjustment.
D&A/SBC reconciliation observations do not become expense/primitive approvals.

The literal AAPL FY2025 regression independently cites its primary table 26:

111,482 USD millions; difference zero, aggregate rounding bound 5.5m. This
statement equation is not an adjusted cash-flow calculation. Four Linde displays
exposed a parent/subtotal double-counting defect in the first enumeration; an
explicit income-parent guard now retains those hierarchies partial without
changing their amounts or pretending a residual proves completeness.
