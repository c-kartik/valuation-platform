## Integration, occurrence selection and failure contract

Use an immutable explicitly published three-version catalog with exact filing/
annual dispatch. Reject duplicate/overlapping active keys, unsupported or unknown
versions/classes, malformed identity and incomplete entry/review models. Do not
choose the highest version, use ticker defaults, trust arbitrary caller policies
or turn research approval flags into executable registration. Add a separate
five-entry Visa runtime registry/model specification; do not masquerade as CVX
or widen its canonical five-entry validator. Preserve existing v1 default/API.

The bounded implementation touches only curated Capex policy/model/registry
dispatch, registered-artifact composition and published-version provenance
validation. SEC retrieval stays outside normalization. Verify all required
bytes and re-parse the verified instance, comparing its facts/context/unit
links with the supplied parsed evidence. Keep `filing_xbrl`, generic historical
selection/resolution, `CAPEX_POLICY` and other measures unchanged. No new cache.

1. Run direct Capex first; preserve direct chosen concepts and ambiguities.
   Unregistered/future/10-K/A filings retain legitimate direct behavior without
   v3 artifact retrieval or implicit approval. Selected filing authority cannot
   be overridden by supplying a reviewed accession.
2. Missing delivered mandatory evidence makes curated acceptance unavailable,
   not zero or resolved. Incomplete published models fail construction. Digest/
   byte-length swaps, wrong-accession artifacts, inconsistent source/registrant/
   selected metadata, malformed or nonfinite numerics, duplicate context/unit
   definitions and missing/broken links are structural data errors. Surface them
   rather than hiding them behind a valid direct result; preserve v1/v2 behavior.
3. Structurally valid wrong entity/annual period/namespace/concept, dimensioned
   or non-USD facts are excluded with ordered reasons, not confirming/conflicting
   operands. Scope/entity/period/unit differences never collapse. Nil-only target
   facts cannot resolve. A nil/numeric collision in the same otherwise eligible
   target scope is a structural error; an excluded dimensional nil does not
   poison a valid nondimensional fact. Never treat an absent item as zero.
4. Group eligible nondimensional occurrences by namespace/taxonomy/concept,
   normalized CIK, accession, authoritative start/end, empty dimensions, exact
   normalized USD, exact finite Decimal and non-nil status. Equivalent context
   IDs, USD unit IDs or lexical/decimals representations may confirm only when
   every semantic field agrees. Preserve every representation in document order;
   parser occurrences are never dropped or summed. No tolerance or rounding.
5. Exactly one signature matching the frozen positive expected Decimal may
   approve. No eligible fact or one changed/negative value leaves fallback
   missing with a mismatch diagnostic. Multiple eligible values remain ambiguous
   even if one matches the reviewed value. No first-value/maximum-value selection,
   absolute-value repair, averaging or residual. Source-body changes ordinarily
   fail integrity first; selection adversaries do not bypass that gate.
6. With complete approved evidence, equal direct/current and curated values
   confirm: retain the direct concept and attach supporting v3 provenance.
   Differing valid values remain ambiguous. Unavailable/unapproved evidence
   cannot replace/confirm a direct value, and direct ambiguity is never repaired.
7. Acquisition/PPA, separate intangibles, book technology, unpaid purchases,
   leases, commitments and proceeds remain ordered typed support/exclusions,
   not operands or implicit adjustment terms. Partial periods, unregistered or
   amended/future filings and incomplete evidence gain no scope approval.
