The workpaper starts with source-bound EBIT, reported tax provision and pretax income, reported cash-statement D&A, actual signed cash movements and cash PP&E. It separately identifies SBC and net nonoperating income under the reported effective-tax allocation convention.

Cash FCF = tax-timing diagnostic + SBC + allocated after-tax net nonoperating income + (tax provision − cash taxes)

Each year ties exactly using rational arithmetic. The tax provision minus cash-tax conversion is observable, but subtracting it from an aggregate does not identify which mixed caption contains tax, noncurrent assets, capital creditors or nonoperating adjustments.

Methodology and reproducibility

Historical FCFF: CFO + gross expensed interest × (1 − reported provision / pretax income) − stated cash PP&E. Historical FCFE: CFO − stated cash investment + identified net borrowing. Meta retains the previously approved identified disposal-receipt corrections. These are source-supported derived measures with declared conventions, not separately audited filing line items.

The CFA formulation supports both CFO and operating-income approaches; it does not supply missing issuer components. The consolidated valuation / minority-interest guidance supports a matching outside-interest deduction, not book-value substitution.
