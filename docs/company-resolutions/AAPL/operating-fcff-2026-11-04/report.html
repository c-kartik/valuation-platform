Important refinement: tax timing remains included

The initial plan preferred separating income-tax balances from operating working capital. Source review found that FY2020–21 current tax balances are not separately disclosed. Removing tax only from later years would change the population mid-series. The implemented refinement retains current tax accruals inside the aggregate current-working-capital definition in every year. It is a documented modeling convention, not a claim that mixed captions are individually verified operating cash flows.

Current tax payable increases by 17.782B in FY2024 and falls by 13.585B in FY2025. Those movements increase and decrease this series respectively. They are material and should be understood before using either year as a recurring forecast base. Removing current taxes where disclosed would change FY2024 to 97.898B and FY2025 to 108.709B; that tax-excluded series is not adopted because the early-year split is unavailable.

Working-capital definition and six-date continuity

NWC = current assets − cash − current marketable securities − current liabilities + commercial paper + current term debt + current finance-lease liability + current operating-lease liability

Other noncash current assets and nondebt current liabilities remain in the aggregate convention. This is a balance-sheet change model, not an assertion that the change is the exact reported cash-statement movement. It excludes noncurrent movements; it does not quietly assign an unexplained cash-flow residual to working capital.

D&A and investment qualifications remain visible

For FY2024, later-note PP&E depreciation 8.2B plus intangible amortization 3.6B totals 11.8B, versus the cash-statement D&A 11.445B. The 0.355B difference remains unexplained. The adopted method deliberately uses the reported combined cash-statement population, consistently, and does not fill the difference. This is not certification of the broader expense-aligned economic-investment measure.

Cash PP&E is the stated investment perimeter. Noncash financed additions are not fabricated or double-counted; finance-lease principal is not a second capital-spending deduction. Exact expanded investment, intangible cash flows and lease timing remain separate analysis. Later comparative notes.

What this resolves—and what it does not

Resolved: the current Apple historical FCFF display now has five source-derived values under one declared operating-income methodology, including FY2024 and FY2025. No missing interest was invented.

Apple’s overall financial review remains OPEN. Full FCFE financing classification, the stricter exact unlevered-cash reconstruction, broader D&A/investment alignment and per-share valuation are not certified by this scoped change. The old missing-interest observations remain null in the preserved legacy series. No Meta/Alphabet cash flow or forecast assumption is changed.
