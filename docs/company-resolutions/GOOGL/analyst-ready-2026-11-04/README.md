# Alphabet — five-output analyst consumer: sources and treatments

## Current acceptance and platform output

All five named outputs are **ANALYST_READY_WITH_QUALIFICATIONS** following source, arithmetic, isolated API, frontend and independent acceptance checks. Open the [implementation report](report.html), [validation record](validation.json) and [independent review](independent-review.md). The selected-case live DCF panel exposes editable assumptions and both share estimates; five/ten-period forecasts and all three operating scenarios are checked. Exact historical cash/ownership reconstruction is not claimed.

The user explicitly requested all five outputs for Meta and Alphabet under the
practical analyst standard already adopted for Apple. This record extends that
named consumer contract; it does not rewrite frozen source facts, claim exact
parent-common historical cash, authorize the wider universe or publish a site.
The source/policy projection is reproducible. Overall five-output readiness is
established by the shared implementation and independent review, not by source
projection alone.

The five outputs are five-year historical FCFF, FCFF forecast/enterprise value,
five-year standardized historical FCFE, FCFE forecast/equity value, and fair value
per share under both approaches. Reported facts, arithmetic, analyst assumptions
and estimates remain separately identified. Existing exact-reconstruction gaps
remain visible as accepted qualifications where a defensible analyst convention
permits a useful output. Implementation/arithmetic errors and hidden assumptions
remain blockers.

FCFE = reported CFO − cash PP&E + identified debt receipts − debt repayments.
Minority equity capital, repurchases, dividends and equity award tax settlements
are not debt funding. These statements have no separate unclassified Other
financing caption; the policy's residual field is 0 **because no such caption is
present**, not proof of no economically unidentified financing components.
Finance-lease financing cash contained in debt repayments is never deducted twice.

Historical source dates are FY2021–25, with the valuation origin **2025-12-31**.
Macro/forecast information may be later, so these are later-informed analytical
estimates, not contemporaneous backtests or current-date price targets.

`policy.json` includes exact retained primary inline IDs, contexts, source URLs,
body hashes and upstream workpaper hashes. Cash, debt, leases, shares and awards
are rebound to the retained FY2025 filing. CFO, cash PP&E and SBC are independently
reselected in each original annual primary body, and five financing calculations
are reconciled to the prior frozen classification schedule. Existing unknowns in
prior ownership/lease schedules remain retained; analyst assumptions do not
silently change their status into reported facts.
