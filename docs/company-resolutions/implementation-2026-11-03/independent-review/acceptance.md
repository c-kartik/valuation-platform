# Independent acceptance contract — November 3, 2026

Scope: AAPL, META and GOOGL, FY2021–FY2025, FCFF and FCFE: 30 requested
historical cells. This audit does not implement issuer or runtime changes.

Each cell passes only if its required inputs are present, independently bound to
official source bytes with compatible period, unit and population, recomputed
without trusting published totals, published correctly in the actual API and
frontend from the reviewed pinned bundle, and free of unresolved hard blockers
for its requested metric, accounting basis and ownership. Arithmetic, software
containment and full financial coverage are separate verdicts.

The approved reported-CFO/expensed-interest/reported-ETR/cash-PP&E FCFF series is
assessed on that explicit consolidated historical convention. Tax provision /
pretax income allocates tax to interest; it is not a reported actual tax shield.
META investment uses gross cash purchases less exact identified PP&E receipts.
No SBC reversal, extra working-capital deduction, additional lease interest,
capitalized-interest addition, noncash investment addition or normalized tax is
silently introduced. Apple requires either the missing exact interest or a
fully reconciled approved five-year alternative; diagnostic EBIT arithmetic is
insufficient. FY2024–25 remain unavailable until that condition is met.

FCFE requires CFO less the matching cash investment plus completely classified
net debt funding/principal, with actual lease cash counted once. Unknown Other
is unknown. AAPL/META partial-financing calculations can pass arithmetic and
honest provisional presentation but cannot pass final FCFE coverage. Required
parent-common ownership is separately tested: GOOGL consolidated FCFE can pass
its consolidated source/arithmetic check but cannot pass the requested
parent-common cell absent source-supported cash allocation. NCI contributions,
book NCI, immaterial minority earnings and enterprise residuals cannot supply
that allocation. Their absence is not a zero or a scope waiver.
