# Apple, Meta and Alphabet — open FCFF/FCFE roadblocks

As of November 3, 2026. **All three company reviews and the combined development
scope remain OPEN.** This register inventories the unresolved items documented in
the existing company decisions, results, limitations and platform handoff. It is
not a fresh search of every filing and does not assert that undiscovered issues
cannot exist. It preserves the original FY2021–FY2025 scope.

The user asks for preferred treatments and requires no completion claim while
any roadblock remains. Treatments below are **proposed recommendations** unless
already approved in the linked issuer decision. This inventory does not change
financial facts, tax rates, valuation assumptions, prior approvals or runtime
numbers. Implemented presentation/receipt/containment treatments are preserved;
they do not close the remaining items below.

## What the classifications mean

- **Historical input:** prevents a final number for the named basis and ownership
  scope. A provisional calculation is not financial clearance.
- **Alternative method / comparison:** limits a proposed replacement series or
  comparison. It need not invalidate an available, accurately labeled historical
  CFO-based series.
- **Forecast / valuation:** concerns forward assumptions or common-share value;
  it does not make an existing historical reported cash number disappear.
- **Development:** missing consumer, integration or validation. Evidence research
  alone cannot close it.

Every item is OPEN. Closure requires the named evidence/policy, source-bound
before/after calculations when amounts change, manual approval where required,
implementation and relevant passing checks. An unknown amount remains unknown;
an approved convention cannot be represented as a separately reported filing fact.

## Method references and interpretation

[CFA Institute, Free Cash Flow Valuation](https://www.cfainstitute.org/insights/professional-learning/refresher-readings/2026/free-cash-flow-valuation)
supports CFO and EBIT routes: CFO-based FCFF adds after-tax interest and deducts
fixed investment; CFO-based FCFE deducts that investment and adds net borrowing.
It distinguishes WACC discounting for FCFF from cost-of-equity discounting for
FCFE and notes that statement interpretation can be nontransparent. It does not
provide an issuer-specific waiver for missing Apple/Meta/Alphabet disclosures.

[Damodaran, Free Cash Flow in Valuation](https://people.stern.nyu.edu/adamodar/pdfiles/blog/FreeCF.pdf),
page 14, distinguishes reported history from a forward valuation base and discusses
recurring normalization, SBC and taxes. This supports a separately reviewed
valuation adjustment layer rather than rewriting the reported cash statement.

[Damodaran, Getting to Equity Value per Share](https://pages.stern.nyu.edu/adamodar/New_Home_Page/littlebook/valuepershare.htm)
supports deducting the estimated market value of outside minority interests when
valuing from consolidated cash flows. It does not make book NCI a directly
observed market value or provide annual parent-common cash attribution.

The preferred treatments in this register are project recommendations informed
by those principles and the existing issuer evidence. They are not represented
as CFA-mandated company-specific adjustments. Historical source facts,
source-supported calculations, accounting conventions and valuation estimates
must retain separate labels.

## Closure and communication rule

Keep the three issuer reviews and combined development status OPEN. Report exact
achievements such as "five financing totals reconcile" or "the identified PP&E
receipt correction is applied." Do not equate approval, successful arithmetic,
passing source checks or a published software component with overall completion.
When an item is resolved, retain its original issue, approval, evidence, exact
before/after effect, implementation and validation; keep the parent OPEN while
any other roadblock remains. Do not silently close an item through a materiality
assumption, scope relabeling or moving it to a different file.
