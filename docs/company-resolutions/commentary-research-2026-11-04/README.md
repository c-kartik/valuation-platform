## Shared items: what commentary can and cannot solve

- **S1:** Keep reported-CFO history and valuation-adjusted history as separate
  outputs. SBC, taxes, investment and other differences need a reviewed bridge
  before automatic calibration or a cross-basis CAGR. Do not suppress a correctly
  labeled historical observation while unrelated valuation work remains open.
- **S2:** Notes can support historical financing and guidance; they cannot report
  future borrowing policy, a uniquely correct beta, equity risk premium, cost of
  equity or WACC. Keep those dated market inputs and explicit model assumptions.
  The manual FCFE engine exists; issuer economics still require review.
- **S3:** Use the existing source-bound cash/claims/share/rights/awards schedule.
  Propose a documented economic-rights allocation and existing-award treatment;
  do not treat EPS weighted-average shares as a dated outstanding-share population
  or assume an outside-claims value of zero.
- **S4:** Separate historical availability, ownership attribution, forecast
  comparability and per-share readiness. Preserve the original failed financial
  review rather than calling a new definition a retroactive pass.

These treatments are project recommendations. CFA's public summary supports CFO
and EBIT formulations and distinguishes cost-of-equity from WACC discounting; it
also notes that necessary statement information may be nontransparent. It does
not provide issuer-specific missing-value waivers. Damodaran's consolidated-value
guidance supports a market-value outside-interest adjustment; it supplies no
missing Apple/Meta transaction amount or exact Alphabet ownership percentage.
