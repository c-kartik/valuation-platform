The [Scope 2 plan](scope-2-research-dashboard.md) records new consumers after the
completed three-company Scope 1 DCF contracts. These are specifications to resolve
before implementation, not new financial values or amendments to accepted models.

  source/retrieval/review dates and valuation origins; preserve restatement vintages.

  steps/points are not invented quarterly/monthly data. Historical FCFF/FCFE keep
  existing issuer/ownership/estimate qualifications; ROIC/TTM/net-debt definitions
  require separate review before exposure.
- Return drivers use P = EPS × P/E with matched positive EPS, share/split basis
  and information cutoff; reconcile multiplicatively. Separate dividend-inclusive
  performance and any later approved additive/log interaction attribution.
- Initial event studies propose benchmark-adjusted daily AR = stock return minus
  benchmark return, CAR = sum of AR, predefined trading-day windows and sourced
  announcement/session timing. Benchmark, window/return conventions and any later
  alpha/beta estimation policy remain to be selected. Report exclusions and sample
  limits; do not claim a validated trading strategy from three issuers.
- Dip-finder discount (V−P)/V, upside (V−P)/P, drawdown P/H−1 and threshold
  V×(1−m) have distinct denominators/meanings. V is positive equity value per
  matching share, never enterprise value. Primary FCFF and secondary FCFE remain
  separate. No company threshold/default margin is approved by the examples.
- Current-date dip classifications require a new valuation-date/elapsed-period/
  dated-bridge/freshness contract. Preserve existing fiscal-end valuations and
  their later-filed-information labels. Historical curves require separately
  accepted dated vintages/reconstruction, never backfilled current assumptions.

The [market contract](market-data-contract.md) and per-issuer acceptance records

## Operating-Company Methodology Scope

The initial valuation framework is designed for ordinary operating companies:

```text
Revenue
→ EBIT
→ NOPAT
+ D&A
− Capex
− Change in Operating NWC
= FCFF
```

This structure is appropriate for many technology, consumer, industrial,
communications, healthcare, and other non-financial operating businesses. It
is not automatically appropriate for every industry.

For banks, deposits and wholesale funding are operating inputs, interest is a
core operating result, conventional operating NWC is not comparable, and
regulatory capital is central. Insurers require specialized treatment of
reserves, claims, float, investment portfolios, underwriting economics, and
regulatory capital. REITs and other specialized structures may also require
dedicated normalization and valuation methods. Those methods are not defined
in the current milestone, and such companies must not be forced into the
generic FCFF framework.

Historical support remains evidence-driven. A supported pipeline run need not
resolve every metric: missing remains missing, ambiguity remains ambiguity,
and methodology blockers remain explicit with filing provenance intact.

## Manual Forecast Assumptions

The initial forecast design uses five explicit ordinal periods rather than
invented calendar dates. Each period is identified by an index from 1 through
5 and a descriptive fiscal-year label, allowing calendar, June, September, and
52/53-week issuers to use the same assumption model.

All rates are manually supplied finite `Decimal` fractions. Each year contains
revenue growth, operating margin, forecast tax rate, D&A as a percentage of
revenue, Capex as a positive expenditure percentage of revenue, and change in
Operating NWC as a percentage of revenue. Positive forecast ΔNWC will later be
a use of cash; negative ΔNWC will be a source of cash. Direct ΔNWC assumptions
avoid making unresolved historical O-NWC a forecast-readiness blocker.
Rate inputs must already be finite `Decimal` instances; integers, floats,
strings, and other numeric representations are rejected rather than coerced.

The assumption set contains one constant manual WACC and one terminal-growth
rate with terminal growth strictly below WACC. It does not calculate Gordon
Growth terminal value. Historical reported ETR, tax expense, and operating-tax
readiness do not populate the manual forecast tax rate. Loss-period tax
treatment must be defined explicitly before a future forecast calculator can
calculate NOPAT for negative EBIT.

Historical diluted weighted-average shares are not a valuation-share policy.
Valuation share count, cash/debt adjustments, and the enterprise-to-equity
bridge remain separate future methodology decisions.

### Signed Tax Reconciliation Evidence

The signed tax-evidence layer records what a selected filing's reconciliation
reports. Each displayed sign requires explicit evidence from the filing table
or a reliable calculation relationship; raw XBRL magnitude is retained
separately and never determines that sign. Rate bridges preserve percentage
points, pretax income, and the Decimal tax amount derived from the displayed
rate. Dollar bridges preserve the filing's signed currency amounts. Both retain
row order and validate against the reported ETR or provision using disclosed
precision, without adding a synthetic balancing row. Because the evidence model
does not identify which displayed terms were rounded, its tolerance conservatively
counts the starting value, each row, and the reported total at the declared
precision. Normalized rows enforce source identity and rate/dollar arithmetic;
signs supported by a calculation relationship require an auditable rationale.

Missing evidence, ambiguous signs, competing sources, conflicts, and unresolved
methodology remain explicit. This layer does not approve operating-tax
treatment, calculate operating tax or NOPAT, or populate a forecast tax
assumption. Reported ETR remains an accounting diagnostic.

### Historical Operating-Tax Attribution

Historical operating-tax methodology uses strict direct linkage. A complete
jurisdictional or reconciliation adjustment may enter tax on reported Operating
Income only when its tax effect is explicitly linked to income inside reported
Operating Income or the filing supplies an exact reproducible operating
allocation. Paired gross-tax and credit effects also require complete pair
membership, evidence of a common regime and income base, and explicit Operating
Income linkage. Any adjustment that does not meet these conditions remains
`METHODOLOGY_UNRESOLVED`.

A recurring caption, a state or foreign label, net presentation, or the absence
of disclosed contamination does not establish operating attribution. The
methodology also prohibits proportional allocation using Operating Income and
Pretax Income, revenue or geographic allocation without a matching disclosed
tax base, and applying a reconciliation percentage directly to Operating
Income.

Percentage-point reconciliation rows are first dollarized using their reported
Pretax Income denominator. Because that denominator can include interest,
investment, financing, and other non-operating income or expense, the resulting
tax amount cannot generally be transferred to Operating Income. Missing
historical operating tax is not estimated to complete a valuation.

Historical operating tax and historical NOPAT are optional analytical outputs.
Reported Income Tax Expense, Reported ETR, signed reconciliation evidence, and
typed operating-tax readiness remain available even when strict linkage cannot
produce an operating-tax amount. The current validation corpus may legitimately
remain methodology-blocked. Forecast FCFF instead uses a separately entered
forecast operating tax-rate assumption; historical operating-tax or NOPAT
availability is not a prerequisite for forecast assumptions or DCF readiness.

### D&A timing basis — Wave 23 methodology decision

The earlier primitive named qualifying asset classes but did not fully specify
manufacturing capitalization timing. Wave 21 required an expense/inventory
bridge and Wave 22 did not establish it. For **new scope/derivation approvals**,
the reviewed target is recurring qualifying D&A **recognized in consolidated
continuing operating income in the authoritative annual period**, after the
related expense reductions actually recognized there. This makes the prior
expense-scope acceptance gate explicit; it is not a change to runtime policies
or retrospective certification of their timing basis. Asset depreciation
generated in the period, cash-flow reconciliation adjustments and balance-sheet
accumulated-depreciation movements are not automatically this expense measure.

Manufacturing depreciation allocated to unsold inventory is deferred; its
qualifying amount enters operating expense when inventory is sold, while
qualifying directly expensed costs enter income in their recognition period.
Current generated depreciation and expense can therefore differ. A complete
bridge must distinguish opening inventory releases, current capitalization,
closing deferral, directly expensed amounts and excluded charges without
double counting. No allocation is inferred from total inventory movements.

This expense basis is **not by itself an approved historical FCFF bridge**:
the existing Operating NWC convention uses book closing-minus-opening levels.
Book inventory can include noncash capitalized depreciation. Adding only
income-expensed D&A while subtracting an unadjusted book inventory increase
can retain that noncash investment in the apparent cash outflow. Conversely,
adding generated depreciation alongside an expense-only inventory movement
can overstate cash flow. An alternative generated-cost basis would require
an explicit compatible EBIT/NWC/Capex/incentive design and versioned impact
assessment, not a relabeling of the normalized expense primitive. Neither an
inventory adjustment nor that alternative is approved or implemented here.
Forecast and valuation work remains paused; normalized availability must not
be mistaken for demonstrated manufacturing FCFF readiness.

Asset-related incentives reduce depreciable basis and subsequent recognized
expense under TXN's disclosed policy; activity-related incentives offset the
related expense. Use an expense net of its demonstrated reductions, not gross
depreciation plus a grant receivable. A reported incentive amount is not a new
D&A operand or authority to subtract twice. Cash timing, tax treatment, Capex
netting and operating-NWC classification require an integrated future review.

**Impact:** TXN 2021–2023 remain further research with no approved equation.
Generic direct, MSFT and five Visa policies and all production counts are
preserved. This documentation review does not prove that every existing
accepted amount is expense-basis aligned; a bounded basis/consumer audit is
required before propagating this clarification into policies, schemas or FCFF.
No automatic invalidation, blanket approval or zero-allocation assumption is
authorized. See the [period decisions and impact assessment](depreciation-amortization-component-derivation-design.md#wave-23--txn-methodology-review).

The core valuation methodology is unlevered free cash flow to the firm (FCFF).

```text
NOPAT = EBIT × (1 - Tax Rate)

FCFF = NOPAT
       + D&A
       - Capital Expenditures
       - Change in Operating NWC
```

For forecast FCFF, the tax rate is a manual analytical assumption rather than
an automatic substitution of Reported ETR or an estimate manufactured from
methodology-blocked historical reconciliation rows. Historical operating tax
and NOPAT may supplement analysis when strict direct linkage resolves, but are

## Explicit FCFE forecast and ownership — November 3

The approved manual alternative keeps operating inputs and funding separate:

```text
Unlevered tax = max(EBIT, 0) × operating tax assumption
Equity tax = max(EBIT − interest expense, 0) × operating tax assumption
Realized interest tax shield = unlevered tax − equity tax
Net borrowing = cash debt issued + matched new finance-asset funding − cash principal repaid
FCFE = FCFF − interest expense + realized interest tax shield + net borrowing
     = net income + D&A − operating investment − working-capital cash needs + net borrowing
```

New financed assets cannot exceed matching operating investment. Debt rolls
forward explicitly; cash principal includes any finance-lease principal once.
Repayments cannot exceed opening debt plus new financing. No tax benefit on a
loss is invented. These are forecast conventions and user assumptions, not a
claim that the corresponding historic financing scope has been established.

FCFE discounts at cost of equity, never WACC. The initial perpetual-growth route
uses independent terminal operating inputs and explicitly supplied terminal
financing. Its declared stable capital structure requires terminal net borrowing
to equal final debt times terminal growth, and positive sustainable terminal
FCFE. Terminal FCFE divided by cost of equity less growth gives terminal equity
value. Debt is not subtracted again. Exit equity multiples and automatically
certified per-share values are not implemented by this route.

CAPM cost of equity = dated risk-free rate + beta × equity risk premium.
WACC = market equity weight × cost of equity + market debt weight × pre-tax cost
of debt × (1 − tax assumption). Code helpers preserve decimal precision; they do
not turn assumed beta, market weights, tax shields or debt costs into filing
facts. Issuer-specific source/date and economic review remain required.

The UI defaults to consolidated scope before minority allocation. A user may
explicitly declare parent-common scope as an assumption; this is not independent
certification or a cure for missing annual attribution. Eligible non-operating
assets, claims, class rights, outstanding awards and dated shares still require
separate reviewed treatment for a share-value output.

Historical reported-CFO calculations retain their own tax, SBC and cash-investment
basis. A partial reported-CFO-minus-reported-SBC-add-back schedule does not by
itself establish valuation-aligned FCFF or FCFE. No missing historical value is
replaced with forecast assumptions. The full three-company review stays OPEN.
