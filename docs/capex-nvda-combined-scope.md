## What the selected notes establish—and do not establish

R10 distinguishes PP&E from separate intangible assets. The PP&E table includes
land, buildings/improvements/furniture, equipment/compute hardware/software and
construction in process. The intangible policy identifies acquired developed
technology and customer relationships, plus technology-license rights, patents
and acquired IP (and IPR&D in the earlier policies). The intangible tables
separately show acquisition-related assets and patents/licensed technology.
Software appearing in PP&E does **not** move those separate legal-right/technology
assets into PP&E. Revenue-side software licensing is not purchase evidence.

The cash-flow caption establishes a combined purchase perimeter. It does not
identify which patent/license/IP transactions were paid within that line in
each year, or quantify them completely. The notes identify asset classes, not
an exhaustive annual cash-payment mapping. Do not state that every intangible
class was purchased for cash in every year or that all intangible transactions
are inside the combined line. Business/transaction consideration is separately
reported, and FY2026 Groq is a specific counterexample to the latter claim.
