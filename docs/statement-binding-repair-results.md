## One shared diagnostic and implementation pass

All fifty-nine prior partial cases receive a cause disposition, not a new issuer
financial review. Shared fixes are bounded to independently supported layouts:

- An opening parenthesis split from its adjacent closing cell is joined only when
  both punctuation cells actually exist. No cash sign is inferred.
- Nested inline tags retain their common visible text. Physically nested standard
  NetIncomeLoss/ProfitLoss tags may confirm one displayed starting population;
  unrelated equal-valued concepts are not aliases.
- The independently tagged current statement row selects its exact matching
  instance amount **and precision**. Other same-concept populations remain explicit
  competing occurrences; this does not approve expense/primitive equivalence.
- A standard total-adjustments parent is usable only with an explicit displayed
  adjustments heading, a complete bound child partition and a separate successful
  child-to-parent check. Parent and children are never summed together.
- Unclassed common-share captions may precede or follow the inline number. Actual
  dates/units/context remain exact; classed or ambiguous populations stay unresolved.
- Explicit current-assets versus noncurrent-assets captions qualify only the
  bounded standard asset pair; broad "other assets" captions still cannot prove
  non-overlap. This does not certify operating NWC or acquisition/FX comparability.

Recognized leases remain separate from borrowing and lease cash service. Broad
short-term investments, uncertain debt basis/inclusion and incomplete share-class
coverage are not promoted to complete fields. No numerical issuer patch, financial
fallback, economic adjustment or valuation calculation is introduced.
