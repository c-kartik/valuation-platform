## Decision: clarification, not expansion

**PARTIAL GO for five exact-accession reported-value scope designs.** The
existing primitive is gross cash expenditure to acquire/construct recognized
PP&E, not a tangible-materials-only measure. Cash costs of purchased and
internally developed operational software are eligible **within Visa's exact
evidenced property/equipment/technology asset and cash perimeter**. This is an
application of the existing asset-and-cost definition, consistent with the
[CVX construction/capitalized-input clarification](capex-cvx-acquisition-perimeter.md),
not a rule that any capitalized software, productive asset or intangible is Capex.

The selected policy notes place purchased/developed software in this operational
asset class, expense preliminary-stage costs and capitalize qualifying
application-development costs. The property note independently identifies
technology beside land, buildings, equipment and assets awaiting use. The
cash-flow statement independently reports purchases of that perimeter and
separately reports business acquisitions, investments and unpaid purchases.
Separate intangible notes identify customer relationships, trade names and
reacquired rights. Together these establish scope; captions, book classification
or numeric agreement alone would not.

An economic asset need not be physically tangible for its evidenced operational
software cost to be within this particular PP&E/technology cost perimeter.
The question is the reported asset and cash scope, not the word "technology."
No expansion is proposed or needed for these five operands. Inclusion of
general purchased rights, all intangibles, standalone subscriptions/hosting,
business purchases or broader economic reinvestment would be an expansion or
a different scope requiring its own impact assessment; none is approved here.

### Cash treatment and acquisition-origin assets

| Item | Current-primitive treatment and evidence limitation |
| --- | --- |
| Purchased capitalized operational software | Eligible only inside the evidenced reported property/equipment/technology cash purchase. Do not add future license commitments or software balances. |
| Internally developed software | Eligible capitalized application-development cash costs within that same component, as construction/development of the operational asset. Preliminary-stage/other expensed costs are not additional Capex. No internal-labor or service-cost residual. |
| Technology received through a business acquisition | Its fair-value recognition changes asset balances; actual acquisition cash can occur in the same year but belongs to the separate business-acquisition line. Do not say acquisitions are cashless, subtract PPA technology from ordinary purchases, or add acquisition cash to Capex. Subsequent ordinary eligible purchase/development cash of consolidated entities is not disqualified merely by their acquisition history. |
| Other acquired intangibles/rights | Separate customer relationships, trade names and reacquired rights do not become PP&E by association. No standalone intangible-only fallback or zero-payment assumption. |
| Unpaid purchases and ROU additions | Noncash recognition is not cash expenditure. ROU assets in Other assets, lease payments and financing principal are not added. No integrated lease model is certified. |
| Proceeds, incentives and software commitments | Preserve the purchase outflow; do not net separate investing inflows, infer a pre-incentive gross-up, invent a grant allocation or treat future minimum payments as current cash. |

The acquisition-origin language describes **book composition**, not overlap of
the two separately reported cash components. FY2022 Tink's $245m technology
PPA, the mixed Currencycloud/Pismo/Featurespace allocations and pending deals
are not current ordinary software-payment operands. FY2023's reported zero
acquisition cash is a fact, not proof of zero software cash. There is no
acquisition adjustment equation.

**Why complete reported-value acceptance, but no equation:** all components of
the independently reported property/equipment/technology cash purchase belong
to the evidenced eligible asset/cost perimeter under this clarification.
A software-versus-hardware cash split is therefore not required to preserve
that whole operand. It remains unquantified and cannot support a technology-only,
tangible-only or acquired-software-adjusted measure. Cash completeness means
this selected reported component, not a claim that every conceivable investment
or future filing has been exhaustively mapped. Separate acquisition cash,
book balances, amortization and commitments are not added or subtracted.

No absence is interpreted as zero. No residual, book movement or D&A approval
proves the cash scope. Display-to-fact checks corroborate signs/values only;
no rounding adjustment is introduced. Scope rests on the independent statement,
accounting, property, intangible and acquisition evidence bound per accession.
