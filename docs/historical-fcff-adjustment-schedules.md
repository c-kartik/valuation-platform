- **Meta FY2025:** total provision 25,474 = current 6,719 + deferred 18,755; net cash taxes 7,578. The earlier 6.63B method test must not be read as a verified cash-FCFF figure. - **Alphabet FY2024:** original net cash taxes 27,400 were reported to 100m precision. FY2025 Note 14 explicitly supplies 27,353 for the same FY2024 population at 1m precision. This schedule selects that later comparative and retains both source accessions/precisions; prior source packages are unchanged.
