## Component findings and decisions

- **PP&E/software:** supplemental notes enumerate land, buildings/improvements,
  machinery/equipment including fleet/software, and construction in progress.
  Policies capitalize qualifying internal-use software, direct external costs,
  project employee costs and development interest in PP&E. This is affirmative
  asset-scope evidence consistent with the existing operational-software
  interpretation, not an exhaustive exact-period cash allocation. Software book
  balances and amortization are not cash components to add or subtract.
- **Intangibles/acquisitions:** intangible notes distinguish franchise rights,
  customer relationships, brands and other identifiable intangibles. The 2021
  cash statement separates acquisition/affiliate investing cash; 2022–2025
  explicitly combine acquisitions/affiliate investments with intangible/other
  asset purchases in a separate line. Neither that aggregation nor its separation
  exhausts the Capital spending perimeter. Acquisition allocation book values,
  goodwill and the Juice Transaction do not establish current cash PP&E operands.
  No acquisition-origin asset or unspecified intangible cash is implicitly added.
- **Leases/noncash:** lease notes explicitly distinguish operating cash payments
  from ROU assets obtained for lease obligations. Later notes state finance leases
  are immaterial, which is not an exact zero. Operating lease cash and noncash
  ROU additions are not cash PP&E purchases. Sale-leaseback gains are not disposal
  cash amounts. No lease adjustment or invented noncash allocation is authorized.
- **Proceeds/netting:** PP&E sales are a separate cash inflow; the issuer's
  non-GAAP free cash flow adds those proceeds back after subtracting spending.
  The project uses gross purchase cash, not that net definition. Do not net
  proceeds, add business sales, or infer missing incentive effects. No exhaustive
  incentive/netting bridge is evidenced for the candidate itself.
- **Timing/overlap:** supplemental payables narratives explicitly mention Capex
  and timing of payments (especially 2024–2025). Book additions and payable
  changes are not independently complete cash bridges. Segment Capital Spending
  totals and investing calculation arcs confirm presentation/arithmetic, not
  economic completeness. Summing segment or repeated facts would double count.

All five periods have the same durable blocker: no exhaustive cash mapping proves
that the reported productive-asset subtotal contains solely eligible PP&E and
capitalized operational-software cash costs, with complete acquisition/intangible,
lease, noncash and netting exclusions. No complete, non-overlapping equation is
supported. This review does not redefine the [Capex primitive](financial-methodology.md)
or retrospectively invalidate its 147 direct and 20 curated results.
