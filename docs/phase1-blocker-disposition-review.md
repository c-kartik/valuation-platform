## Minimal proposed reported-input delivery contract

1. Deliver **facts/gaps at explicitly listed periods**, not a whole-company
   financial-sufficiency claim. Retain the five-period target, 189 selected rows
   and 11 null history slots. Existing accounting closure is not lowered or
   replaced; financial sufficiency stays NOT_ASSESSED.
2. A reviewed reported-input package must bind each supplied fact to exact
   filing/period/source, roles, units, raw value, sign, submitted precision,
   contexts and all eligible occurrences. Retain typed byte digests, actual
   receipt metadata, and refresh/original history separately. A counts-only
   corpus row or verified DEI identity is insufficient for this claim.
3. Require source-bound reported CFO and its **complete displayed indirect
   reconciliation for that subtotal**, plus separately identified cash purchase
   inputs or an explicit missing/mixed-scope requirement. A narrow PP&E claim
   needs affirmative cash/perimeter evidence; a mixed reported line may instead
   remain labeled mixed and unapproved as the Capex primitive. Retain related
   tax, interest, SBC, lease and acquisition/noncash evidence or explicit gaps.
4. Separate cash payments, accrued expense, closing capital creditors, noncash
   additions and reconciliation adjustments. Confirmations count once. A note
   expense and CFO add-back are related roles, not two economic costs; rounded
   and exact reported points are not silently reconciled by an adjustment.
5. Reported movements retain source caption, raw-to-display sign and acquisition/
   FX/recast/current-versus-noncurrent scope. Do not call them reconstructed
   Operating NWC, subtract them again from CFO, or add D&A/SBC again. No new
   movement subtotal, adjusted result or valuation calculation is approved.
6. Analyst assumptions/classifications require exact scope, source or justified
   rationale, author/date, explicit acceptance, precision/uncertainty and prior
   decision history. Where no defensible number exists, deliver an unresolved
   requirement, not a numerical proxy. Forecast assumptions remain Phase 2.

Permitted use is historical inspection, source tracing, separate serialization
and reported-statement validation. It is not organic/continuing-only cash,
complete reinvestment, complete interest/leases/NWC, forecast calibration or
FCFF consumption. The [timing contract](financial-timing-readiness-design.md)
still governs its defined EBIT/D&A/book-NWC consumer. An eventual alternative
consumer would need an explicit compatible cash/tax/lease/SBC convention and
its own evidence gates; do not either impose the book reconstruction requirement
on mere reported-CFO preparation or waive economic readiness automatically.
