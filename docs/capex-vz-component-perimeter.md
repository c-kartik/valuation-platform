## Perimeter and component findings

- **PP&E:** network equipment, buildings, antennas/cable/conduit/poles/towers,
  leasehold improvements, work in progress and other property are stated at cost.
  MD&A describes network capacity/productivity and infrastructure investment.
  Purpose and cost balances do not exhaust exact-period cash purchases.
- **Software/cloud:** policies capitalize internal-use network and non-network
  software and qualifying significant functionality upgrades. Non-network
  software is in Other intangible assets; cloud implementation deferrals are in
  Prepaid expense and other. Planning, maintenance and training are expensed.
  These are distinct accounting categories, not a complete cash decomposition.
  Book additions/amortization or changes in balances cannot be substituted for
  a cash-software operand. An unexplained cloud allocation is not permitted.
- **Existing primitive:** evidenced capitalized operational software can fit the
  [existing cash cost perimeter](financial-methodology.md); its intangible book
  location alone neither rejects nor approves it. Visa's exact reviewed scope
  does not approve all VZ software/cloud/intangible spending. Do not subtract all
  capitalized software merely to manufacture tangible PP&E, or silently expand
  the primitive to all productive assets.
- **Spectrum/licenses:** the cash-flow license line is separate; notes identify
  license transactions and clearing/incentive obligations. Spectrum acquisition
  and spectrum capitalized-interest balances are not ordinary network PP&E cash.
  C-Band network deployment is not the same expenditure as purchasing C-Band
  licenses. Do not subtract an already separate license line from the candidate,
  or infer a cash interest allocation from capitalized book interest.
- **Businesses/disposals:** acquisition receipts/payments and business disposition
  proceeds are outside the reported candidate. These lines are not a complete
  cash component inventory for the candidate itself. Other investing cash is
  aggregated; no missing-as-zero PP&E disposal, incentive or other-intangible
  assumption. The 2026 Frontier/Starry acquisitions described in the 2025 filing
  are subsequent events, not 2025 candidate operands.
- **Leases/noncash:** lease notes distinguish operating cash, finance-lease
  interest/principal and noncash ROU additions. Finance-leased assets appear in
  PP&E without being cash purchases. The primary filing's debt disclosure
  expressly excludes purchases financed by long-term liabilities from cash
  Capex as noncash financing activities. This proves that specific exclusion,
  not all cash timing/netting or component scope. No lease principal, unpaid
  addition or ROU amount is added to or subtracted from the candidate.
- **Overlap/extensions:** all components must be evidenced cash and mutually
  exclusive before any equation; balance-category separation is insufficient.
  Extensions remain separately gated and are not automatically approved from
  labels, local names or related standard concepts.
